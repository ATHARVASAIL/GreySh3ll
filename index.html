The method

An assessment isn't a checklist.
It's a sequence.

Findings compound. What you learn in recon shapes the web tests; a leaked credential in source review unlocks the cloud. GreySh3ll orders every case the way the work actually flows, so nothing is tested out of context.

Map the ground

Network, infrastructure and exposed services first. You can't test what you haven't seen — recon sets the scope for everything that follows.

Work the surfaces

Web, API, cloud, mobile, thick-client, LLM and source code — each with its own standards taxonomy, real payloads and evidence-ready steps.

Close on the human layer

Phishing, pretexting and physical access last — once the technical picture is complete, so social-engineering findings land in full context.
